For grown-ups

Sharing God’s love with little ones

Children under 5 learn about God first through you: your hugs, your routines and the way you talk about him. Short, warm and often is better than long and perfect.

How little ones learn

🤗 Love comes first

For babies and toddlers, a safe, loving grown-up is their first picture of what God is like. Cuddles, comfort and saying “God loves you” at the same time teach the most.

🔁 Repeat, repeat, repeat

Little ones love hearing the same story again and again. That’s how they learn. Read the same story all week if they ask for it.

👐 Bodies and senses

Under 5, children learn by moving, touching, singing and making sounds. That’s why every story has a hand motion and something to do.

🖼️ Pictures and simple words

Short sentences and big, bright pictures work best. They take words literally, so keep it simple: “God made you. God loves you.”

⏱️ Two to five minutes

Attention spans are short, and that’s okay. A little every day beats a long time once a week.

🌙 Routines feel safe

Tie faith to daily rhythms: a bedtime story and blessing, a thank-you before meals, a song in the car.

A simple bedtime rhythm

1 · ReadRead one little story slowly and point to the picture.
2 · SaySay “God loves me!” together. Let them shout it!
3 · DoDo the hand motion together.
4 · PraySay the tiny prayer, and bless your child: “God loves you, and so do I.”
